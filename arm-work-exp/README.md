# 🚀 ARM Satellite Simulation Project

## Overview

As part of an engineering and technology challenge supported by Arm, I worked in a team to design, build and test a protective chassis for a micro:bit device.

The project simulated the challenge of protecting the micro:bit during deployment and impact events in order to measure various data. Our team designed a housing, collected acceleration data during free-fall tests, investigated wireless communications between devices and presented our findings to Arm representatives.

---

## My Responsibilities

During the project, I contributed to:

- Designing and building a protective chassis to house a micro:bit during drop tests.
- Programming the micro:bit buttons to start and stop data collection.
- Recording acceleration measurements during free-fall experiments.
- Presenting the team's process, findings and outcomes to a panel of Arm members.

---

## Technical Activities

### Protective Chassis Design

Our team designed and constructed a protective enclosure for a micro:bit, similar to an egg-drop challenge. The objective was to protect the device while allowing acceleration data to be collected during testing.

### Data Collection and Testing

The micro:bit was programmed to record acceleration values during free-fall tests. This enabled us to analyse how different designs affected impact forces and device protection.

### Wireless Communication

The project involved receiving and decrypting wireless signals transmitted from another micro:bit. This required logical problem-solving and understanding how information can be transmitted and interpreted between devices.

### Presentation and Communication

At the conclusion of the project, we presented our design process, testing results and conclusions to a panel of Arm representatives, explaining both the technical and practical aspects of our work.
