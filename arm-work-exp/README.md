# 🚀 ARM Satellite Simulation Project

## Overview

Team engineering challenge run with support from Arm: design, build, and test a protective chassis for a micro:bit device, simulating the deployment and impact conditions a payload - like a satellite component — has to survive on landing.

Our team built a housing, ran free-fall drop tests while logging acceleration data on the micro:bit, tested wireless communication between two micro:bits, and presented our results to a panel of Arm engineers.

---

## My Contribution

- Designed and built the protective chassis - bamboo sticks were the main material (strength to weight ratio) and built as a hexagon like shape.
- Programmed the micro:bit (Python) so its buttons started and stopped accelerometer logging during drop tests
- Ran the free-fall tests and recorded acceleration data — dropped 4 to 5 times to ensure outliers don't hold weight then extracted the information. 
- Worked on the wireless signal task: receiving and decoding a transmission from a second micro:bit
- Presented the team's design process, results, and conclusions to the Arm panel

---

## Challenges

**Impact protection vs. functionality** — the chassis had to absorb enough force to protect the micro:bit without blocking the accelerometer readings or button access, which meant we had to improvise the interior to allow for the buttons to be exposed.
