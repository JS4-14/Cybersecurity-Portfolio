# NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)

## Overview

The NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) is a widely used framework that helps organisations manage cybersecurity risks.

It was developed by the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST).

---

## Core Functions

### 1. Identify

Understand assets, systems and risks.

Examples:

- Asset inventories
- Risk assessments

---

### 2. Protect

Implement safeguards to reduce risk.

Examples:

- MFA
- Security awareness training
- Access controls

---

### 3. Detect

Identify cybersecurity incidents quickly.

Examples:

- SIEM tools
- Security monitoring

---

### 4. Respond

Take action when an incident occurs.

Examples:

- Incident response plans
- Communication procedures

---

### 5. Recover

Restore systems and operations after an incident.

Examples:

- Backups
- Disaster recovery plans

---

## Why Organisations Use NIST

- Improves risk management
- Provides a structured approach
- Helps strengthen security programs
- Widely recognised across industries

---

## Key Takeaways

- Identify
- Protect
- Detect
- Respond
- Recover

These five functions form the foundation of the NIST Cybersecurity Framework.
