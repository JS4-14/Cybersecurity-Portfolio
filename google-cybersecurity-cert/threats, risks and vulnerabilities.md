# Threats, Risks and Vulnerabilities

## Overview

Threats, risks and vulnerabilities are closely related concepts that security professionals use to assess and manage security issues.

---

## Threat

A threat is anything capable of causing harm to a system, network or organisation.

### Examples

- Cybercriminals
- Malware
- Insider threats
- Natural disasters

---

## Vulnerability

A vulnerability is a weakness that can be exploited by a threat.

### Examples

- Weak passwords
- Outdated software
- Misconfigured systems
- Unpatched applications

---

## Risk

Risk is the potential damage that could occur if a threat successfully exploits a vulnerability.

### Example

Threat:
Attacker

Vulnerability:
Weak password

Risk:
Unauthorised access to company systems

---

## Relationship Between Them

A threat exploits a vulnerability which creates risk.

Example:

- Threat = Hacker
- Vulnerability = Outdated software
- Risk = Data breach

---

## Key Takeaways

- Threats cause harm.
- Vulnerabilities are weaknesses.
- Risks are potential consequences.
- Organisations reduce risk through security controls.
