# Security Controls

## Overview

Security controls are safeguards designed to reduce risk and protect organisational assets.

They help organisations prevent, detect and respond to security threats.

---

## Administrative Controls

Administrative controls are policies and procedures that guide employee behaviour.

### Examples

- Security awareness training
- Acceptable use policies
- Background checks
- Incident response plans

---

## Technical Controls

Technical controls use technology to protect systems and data.

### Examples

- Firewalls
- Antivirus software
- MFA
- Encryption

---

## Physical Controls

Physical controls protect physical assets from unauthorised access.

### Examples

- Security guards
- Locked doors
- CCTV
- Access cards

---

## Why Security Controls Matter

Security controls help:

- Reduce organisational risk
- Protect sensitive data
- Improve compliance
- Detect and prevent attacks

---

## Key Takeaways

- Administrative controls focus on people and processes.
- Technical controls use technology.
- Physical controls protect physical assets.
- Organisations use multiple controls together for stronger security.
