# Risk Management

## Overview

Risk management is the process of identifying, assessing and reducing security risks that could impact an organisation.

The goal is not to eliminate all risk, but to reduce risk to an acceptable level.

---

## Risk Components

A risk is created when:

- A threat exists
- A vulnerability exists
- An asset could be impacted

Example:

Asset: Customer Database

Threat: Cybercriminal

Vulnerability: Weak Password

Risk: Data Breach

---

## Risk Assessment

Risk assessments help organisations identify and prioritise risks.

Typical steps:

1. Identify assets
2. Identify threats
3. Identify vulnerabilities
4. Determine likelihood
5. Determine impact
6. Prioritise risks

---

## Risk Treatment Options

### Mitigate

Reduce the risk.

Example:
Enable MFA.

### Transfer

Move the risk elsewhere.

Example:
Purchase cyber insurance.

### Accept

Accept the risk.

Example:
Low-impact vulnerabilities.

### Avoid

Remove the activity causing risk.

Example:
Disable an insecure service.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Risk cannot be eliminated completely.
- Organisations assess likelihood and impact.
- Risks can be mitigated, transferred, accepted or avoided.
